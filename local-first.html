What local-first actually means here

Not a marketing label. A set of concrete promises about how AssetHoard treats you and your work.

🙅

No account, ever

There is no sign-up screen between you and your assets. No email to verify, no password to reset, no profile we hold over you. Install and go.

🏠

Your assets never leave your computer

AssetHoard reads and previews the files already on your disk. Nothing is uploaded, copied to the cloud, or synced to a server we run.

🗄️

We do not store your library data

Your file names, tags, notes, and thumbnails stay with you. We keep no copy of your catalogue, because we never receive it in the first place.

📡

One licence check every 30 days

The single time AssetHoard needs a server is a brief licence validation roughly once a month. Between checks, feel free to stay offline as long as you like.

🔄

Lifetime updates

We ship improvements regularly, and your licence includes all of them. No subscription, no upgrade tier, no paywall on the features you already paid for.

🔓

No lock-in

Your assets are plain files in your own folders. AssetHoard sits on top of them rather than swallowing them, so you are never trapped.

About that one license check

We believe in being straight with you, so here is exactly when AssetHoard talks to the license server and what it sends.

How often

A licence check roughly every 30 days. That is it.

What it sends

Only enough to confirm your licence is valid. Not your assets, not your library, not your file names.

Between checks

Nothing. You can disconnect entirely and keep working without interruption.

If you are offline at check time

AssetHoard keeps working and validates the next time you have a connection. You are never locked out mid-project. We'll give you 30 days to come back online, so that's ~60 days between required checks
