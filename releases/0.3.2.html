v0.3.2 15 November 2026

Find similar images and export Unity prefabs whole

AssetHoard is a local-first asset manager for game developers and digital artists. Version 0.3.2 adds visual similarity search to find similar images, Unity prefabs that come out of a .unitypackage with everything they need, a licence filter, and update prompts you won't miss.

🔍 Find similar images and sprites

Right-click a texture, sprite, model or material and choose Find Similar to see the assets in your library that look most like it, closest match first. It runs entirely on your machine, using semantic search.

  • Works with your filters. Narrow by type, category, tags or bundle for "more like this, but only PNGs in this pack".
  • Type similar:<asset id> in search, or use it through MCP and the local HTTP API.
  • Needs semantic search switched on with your images indexed.

🎮 Export Unity prefabs with their dependencies

Drag a prefab, material or other member out of a .unitypackage and it brings everything it references in the package: meshes, materials, textures, shaders and scripts. Each file lands at its project path beside its .meta, so Unity keeps the GUIDs and nothing arrives with missing materials. See the Unity docs.

  • Missing pieces are flagged. Anything needed from outside the package shows up in a notice instead of going quietly missing.
  • See what a prefab uses. Prefabs, materials and scenes list their references under Associated files. Click through from a prefab to its materials and on to its textures.

📜 Filter assets by licence

  • Pick one or more licences in the filters (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC BY-NC, MIT, Royalty-Free, Custom or No license). Works in search too, with counts.
  • LICENSE files found on import are read for what they are, so an MIT or CC0 pack lands under MIT or CC0 rather than Custom. More in the licence detection docs.
  • Counts update straight away when you change a licence.

🔔 Updates you won't miss

The update toast now stays put until you choose Install now, Install on exit or Remind me later. Install on exit downloads straight away and installs when you close AssetHoard.

📤 Export and drag

  • Audio in its own format. Export As now offers the original format first, e.g. OGG (original)..., as an exact copy. Thanks to Viral for the nudge.
  • No more "N items" folder. Exporting several models puts each in its own folder, and one failure no longer stops the rest.
  • FBX textures found. Models whose textures point at the author's computer (common in packs like KayKit) now find them in the model's own folder.
  • Dragging a regular bundle alongside a package bundle keeps its folders.

🐛 Fixes

  • Non-square pixel art in TGA, Krita and FreeCAD files keeps its shape in thumbnails.
  • The feedback form fills in your licence email.
  • The import dialog uses the same licence list as the rest of the app, with CC BY-SA added. Older picks like "CC BY 4.0" are tidied up on first launch.
  • Linux: the .deb and .rpm now show the icon and details in GNOME Software.
  • Faster, calmer startup on large libraries.

Happy hoarding.
