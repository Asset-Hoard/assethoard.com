What we will refund

  • Duplicate purchases: if you were charged twice for the same licence, we will refund the duplicate immediately.

What we will not refund

  • Change of mind, personal preference, or subjective dissatisfaction.
  • Features requested that are outside the product's stated scope.
  • Issues arising from environment changes made after purchase, including switching operating systems, running AssetHoard in a virtual machine or remote desktop, or using an unsupported OS version.
  • Refusal to cooperate with reasonable troubleshooting steps from support.

Refund eligibility is assessed based on the device and environment at the time of purchase.

How to request a refund

  • Contact us at support@assethoard.com within 14 days of purchase.
  • Include your licence key or order confirmation and a brief description of the issue.
