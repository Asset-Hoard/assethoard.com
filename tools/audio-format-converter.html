About the Audio format converter

Different engines and platforms prefer different audio formats: WAV for lossless source, MP3 and OGG Vorbis for smaller compressed files. This converter decodes your clips and re-encodes them to the format and quality you need, one file at a time or in a batch.

How to use the Audio format converter

  1. Drop one audio file or a whole batch.
  2. Choose the output format: WAV, MP3 or OGG.
  3. Set the quality for the compressed formats.
  4. Download the converted files.
