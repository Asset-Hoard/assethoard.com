About the Audio waveform trimmer

Recorded and downloaded sound effects often carry silence or unwanted tails around the part you actually want. This trimmer shows the clip as a waveform so you can select the exact region by eye, preview it, and export just that part as a WAV.

How to use the Audio waveform trimmer

  1. Load an audio clip.
  2. Drag on the waveform to select the region you want to keep.
  3. Preview the selection.
  4. Export the trimmed clip as a WAV.
