🎮

Indie Developers

You're juggling code, art, audio, and a backlog of Asset Store purchases you've forgotten about. Asset Hoard gives you one searchable library for everything: prefabs, scripts, sprites, sounds. so you spend less time digging through folders and more time shipping your game.

Key features:

  • Unified library across all asset types
  • Unity and Godot package inspection
  • Bulk tagging and smart organisation
  • Drag-and-drop export to your project
🎨

2D Artists

Your pixel art and illustrations deserve better than a mess of nested folders. Asset Hoard previews your .aseprite and .krita files with animation playback, so you can browse your work visually and tag it your way.

Key features:

  • Animated previews for Aseprite files
  • Krita file support with layer awareness
  • Sprite sheet previews and metadata
  • Custom tagging for characters, tilesets, UI
🧊

3D Artists

Preview your models without opening Blender. Asset Hoard generates thumbnails and interactive previews for .fbx, .obj, .gltf, and .blend files, and keeps your PBR texture sets grouped and organised.

Key features:

  • Rotate and zoom 3D previews
  • Auto-generated thumbnails on import
  • PBR texture set grouping
  • Material and mesh metadata display
🔊

Sound Designers

Audition your entire SFX library without leaving the app. Waveform thumbnails let you spot the right sound visually, and inline playback means no more opening files one by one.

Key features:

  • Waveform thumbnail generation
  • Inline playback with scrubbing
  • Loop vs one-shot detection
  • Tags for mood, intensity, category
