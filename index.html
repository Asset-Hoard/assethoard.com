Everything you need to manage your game assets

Semantic search

Find assets by meaning, not just filename. Search for 'water' and get results even if the file is called IMG_4392.png.

Rich previews

Thumbnails for everything. 3D model rotation, sprite animation playback, audio waveforms, PBR material previews. See what you have without opening another app.

40+ file formats

From Asprite, to GLTF & FBX, to MTLX and MAT, to WAV and OGG, .unitypackage, and more. One library for all of them.

Tags and categories

Custom colour-coded tags, auto-tags by file type and resolution, and smart filters you can save for quick access.

Projects and bundles

Group assets into projects or flexible bundles. Organise by game, by client, or project, whatever works for you.

Audio waveforms

Waveform thumbnails and inline playback with scrubbing. Audition sounds without opening another app.

Visual importer + watched folders

Preview files before importing. Filter and ignore what you do not need, apply tags on the way in, and let smart license parsing detect CC0, MIT, and more.

Local-first and offline

All data stored on your machine. No account, no cloud. Works without an internet connection.

Unity drag and drop

Godot drag and drop
