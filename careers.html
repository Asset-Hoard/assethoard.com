Our Values

Asset Hoard is an intentionally small, bootstrapped company building tools to help create faster, and delightful experience for indie game developers. While we do so, we're striving to create a more humane and sustainable way to work.

Helping our customers succeed is our mission. If you like seeing the direct impact of your work while collaborating with people who care deeply about doing things right by others, Asset Hoard might be a place for you.

🤝

Transparent & Responsible

Being clear, honest, open, and acting with integrity in everything we do.
🎨

Indie Experience

Making tools that developers, artists, and musicians love to use. If it's frustrating, we fix it.
✨

Quality Over Quantity

Shipping polished, reliable software. We'd rather do fewer things well than many things poorly.
🌏

Remote & Async

Work from anywhere, on your schedule. Results matter more than hours in a seat.

Benefits & Perks

We take care of our team

🏠

Work Comfortably

As a fully distributed company, your only commute is the walk to your home office. You will get a laptop of your choice, plus $1,000 to upgrade your home office. We also cover a coworking desk if you prefer getting out of the house.
⏰

Create Your Schedule

We trust you to tailor your workday as long as you're accountable for your work. Do a school dropoff, go to therapy or run an errand!
👨‍👩‍👧

Spend Time With Your Family

All new parents get 12 weeks of paid leave after twelve months of employment, including adoption and foster care.
🌴

Take Time to Refresh

4 weeks of annual leave, 2 weeks of sick leave, your birthday off, volunteer days, plus national, state and territory public holidays.
📚

Growth Mindset

Enjoy $1,000 annually in skill development reimbursements to continue your professional development with books, courses, or anything else to help you learn.
💚

Health and Wellbeing

We contribute to your health insurance and offer a wellness stipend for gym memberships, therapy, or whatever helps you feel your best.
