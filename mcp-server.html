Your asset library, inside your AI assistant

Say "find me a few low-poly rocks and put them in Assets/Props" and your assistant searches the library you've already built in AssetHoard, checks the previews and copies the files into your project. AssetHoard's MCP server makes it possible, and it never leaves your computer.

🔒

Stays on your machine

Listens on 127.0.0.1 only, needs a secret token, and refuses requests from web pages.

👀

Read-only by default

Out of the box the assistant can search, browse, inspect and export. Changing your library is a separate switch.

↩️

Everything undoable

Every change shows a toast and is a normal Ctrl+Z undo entry. Imports wait for you to click Allow.

See it in action

Claude Code is asked for a capybara sprite. It finds the "8 bit Capibaras" pack in AssetHoard, exports the bundle into the project's sprites folder, and the sheets turn up in Godot straight away.

What you can ask for

Stay in your editor or chat. The assistant does the searching and copying against your own library, and replies with links that open each asset in AssetHoard.

"Do I have any mossy stone wall textures?"

Searches by name and, with semantic search on, by meaning. The assistant can look at the thumbnails before answering.

"Copy those three rock models into Assets/Props."

Exports straight into your project folder with their textures. Existing files are skipped, never overwritten.

"Drop my 'Dungeon Tileset' bundle into the Godot project."

Exports the whole bundle, with its sub-bundles as folders and referenced files alongside.

"Find three different barrels and a wood texture that matches."

Set dressing without leaving your editor. The assistant compares previews, then exports the picks.

"Suggest tags for the untagged textures in my Downloads bundle."

With write tools on, it can apply the tags you approve. Every change can be undone with Ctrl+Z.

"Which assets in the Crawler project have no licence recorded?"

Reads your projects and the licence field on each asset, so you can fix gaps before you ship.

What the assistant can do

🔍 Always on

  • Search like the search bar, including by meaning with semantic search
  • Filter by file type, engine, tags, category or bundle
  • Browse bundles, tags, categories and projects
  • Read full asset details, including the licence
  • Look at preview images
  • Export assets and bundles into a folder, with referenced files

🗂️ When you allow write tools

  • Create, rename and move bundles
  • Add and remove tags
  • Create categories and set them on assets and bundles
  • Create projects and add assets to them
  • Import files and folders, with your approval

There's no tool for deleting assets or bundles, and export never overwrites a file.
