Free browser-based tools for game asset work

This is a growing collection of free, single-purpose tools for the everyday jobs that come up when you work with game assets: packing and slicing sprite sheets, generating normal maps, compressing images and 3D models, converting audio, and tidying up sound effects. Each one runs entirely in your browser, so your files stay on your machine. There is nothing to install and no account to create.
