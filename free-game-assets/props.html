Questions about free CC0 props

Most are, meaning they are low-poly or sprite-sized with textures applied and sensible pivots. Check the polygon count before scattering hundreds of them, and combine small props into a single mesh or atlas where your engine benefits.

Yes. CC0 allows commercial use, including paid games. What you cannot do is resell the props themselves as if you made them, since anyone can get them free from the original source.
