Questions about free CC0 environments

A modular kit is a set of pieces such as walls, floors, corners and doors built to a shared grid, so they snap together into many different layouts. It is the fastest way to block out and dress levels.

Yes, and CC0 makes it legally simple. The practical issue is style: match the art style, polygon density and colour palette so the pieces sit together, or unify them with a shared material or palette.
